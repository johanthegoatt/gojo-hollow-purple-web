Blue + Red = Hollow Purple

Hold one hand for Blue, two hands for Blue + Red, bring hands closer to merge, then close your hands and open them to release. Spread fingers to expand Blue/Red, and increase two-hand spacing to expand Purple. Blue is a circle-construction filled with parabolas, Red is a clean red globe, and Purple fuses both with theorem geometry.
